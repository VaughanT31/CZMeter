# CZ Meter wiki

How to use CZ Meter: reading the window, player details, several windows snapped together, skins you can share, the options and the combat log.

Also on the website: [CZ Meter wiki](https://ctrlshiftzed.com/wiki/cz-meter/) | Get it on [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/czmeter)

## Pages

- [Getting started](Getting-started.md): Install it, type /cz, and the meter is there.
- [The window](The-window.md): What each row shows and what the toolbar buttons do.
- [Player details](Player-details.md): Hover a row for a quick look, or click it for that player's spells, targets and defensive numbers.
- [Several windows](Several-windows.md): Open up to six windows and snap them together into a group.
- [Skins](Skins.md): Built-in skins, your own changes, and sharing a skin as a text string.
- [Options](Options.md): Every page of the options, and test bars to try them on.
- [Combat log and disk space](Combat-log-and-disk-space.md): Logging raids and keys automatically, without filling your drive.
- [Commands](Commands.md): Every CZ Meter chat command.
- [Questions and fixes](Questions-and-fixes.md): Answers to the things people ask most.
