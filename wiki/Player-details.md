# Player details

*Hover a row for a quick look, or click it for that player's spells, targets and defensive numbers.*

Click any player's row to open their breakdown for that window's metric and fight.

### Quick look on hover

Hover a row and a short card shows above the window (or below it, near the top of the screen): the player's total, per second, share and rank, and their top 8 spells. Your own card works in combat. Other players' cards show their totals in combat and fill in their spells once combat ends. Turn it off with **Show details when hovering a row** on the Display page.

### Compare to the last pull

After a fight, the details panel and the hover card compare the player with their **previous pull on the same boss**: the change per second (or in total, for metrics without per second), the spell that went up most and the one that went down most, for example *+4.2% vs last pull: Eviscerate +300K, Rupture -100K*. It works for the current fight and any earlier pull you pick from History. It doesn't show in combat, for Overall, best try or the Enemies tab, or on the first pull of a boss.

### Personal bests

After each boss kill, CZ Meter saves your DPS (HPS as a healer) against that boss, difficulty and spec, with the key level in Mythic+. Every dungeon boss counts on its own, which a whole-run score doesn't show. One chat line after the kill says whether you beat your best and by how much, for example *new personal best on Altar Boss (Mythic Keystone): 1.5M DPS, up 25.0% from 1.2M*. Your own row's hover card and details show your best for the boss you last fought, and `/cz pb` lists your bests for the spec you're in. Wipes don't count. Turn the chat line off with **Personal best in chat after a kill** on the Display page.

### The header

The player's spec, name in class colour, the metric and fight, and four numbers: **total**, **per second**, **share** of the group and **rank**.

### Spells

Each ability with its total, per second and share of the player's total. Hover a spell for its overkill and the targets it hit.

### Targets

Damage added up per target, where the game gives a split.

### Defense

Damage taken, avoidable damage, absorbs, healing, interrupts, dispels and deaths, each with the player's rank. Below them are the spells that hit the player hardest, with avoidable ones in red.

### Death recap

On the **Deaths** metric, click a death and the Spells tab shows the last hits before it, newest first: how long before the death, the spell, who cast it, how hard it hit and the health left, with the killing blow in red. Hover a hit for overkill. **Full recap** opens Blizzard's own death recap with every hit. The recap shows once combat ends.

After a wipe, chat lists who died in order, what killed them and when in the fight. Turn it off with **Deaths in chat after a wipe** on the Display page.

> Your own details open any time. Other players' details open once combat ends, because the game keeps their numbers hidden from addons during combat. Details aren't available for enemy rows or for Best.
