# Player details

*Click a row for that player's spells, targets and defensive numbers.*

Click any player's row to open their breakdown for that window's metric and fight.

### The header

The player's spec, name in class colour, the metric and fight, and four numbers: **total**, **per second**, **share** of the group and **rank**.

### Spells

Each ability with its total, per second and share of the player's total. Hover a spell for its overkill and the targets it hit.

### Targets

Damage added up per target, where the game gives a split.

### Defense

Damage taken, avoidable damage, absorbs, healing, interrupts, dispels and deaths, each with the player's rank. Below them are the spells that hit the player hardest, with avoidable ones in red.

> Your own details open any time. Other players' details open once combat ends, because the game keeps their numbers hidden from addons during combat. Details aren't available for enemy rows or for Best.
