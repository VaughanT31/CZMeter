# The window

*What each row shows and what the toolbar buttons do.*

### The rows

Each row shows the player's **rank**, **spec icon**, **name**, **item level**, **total** and **per second**, with **share** of the group as an option. Bars use class colours unless your skin says otherwise.

Your own row is outlined and always on the list, even when you're below the number of rows shown.

Item levels are read out of combat, so new players in the group show theirs after the fight.

### Columns

Open the window menu (the cog) and pick **Columns** to choose which columns show: rank, spec icon, item level, per second and share. Share is off by default, which leaves more room for names.

### The toolbar

- **The arrows and the metric name:** step through the metrics, or click the name to pick one from the list.
- **Current, Overall or History:** the current fight, the overall total, or an earlier pull.
- **Players and Enemies:** Enemies shows how much damage went into each enemy type.
- **My DPS:** only your row.
- **Best:** your group's best pull of the last boss, next to the one you just did.
- **Top:** how many rows to show.
- **Copy:** the numbers as text, to paste into chat.
- **The cog:** the window menu. See below.

**Always show me** (in the window menu, on by default) keeps your own row in sight. If you're below the top rows, or the window is too short to reach you, your row takes the bottom line at your real rank.

On a narrow window the toolbar goes compact, and the buttons that don't fit move into the window menu.

### The window menu

The cog holds **Columns**, **Always show me**, **Clear meter history**, **New window**, **Lock**, **Ungroup**, **Close window** and **Options**.

### Metrics

| Metric | What it counts |
|---|---|
| **Damage** | Damage done |
| **Healing** | Healing done |
| **Absorbs** | Damage your shields absorbed |
| **Damage taken** | Damage each player took |
| **Avoidable damage** | Damage taken that could have been avoided |
| **Interrupts** | Spell casts interrupted |
| **Dispels** | Effects dispelled |
| **Deaths** | Deaths in the fight |
| **Enemy damage** | Damage that went into each enemy (the same as the Enemies tab) |

### Height and scrolling

Windows keep a fixed height and scroll with the mouse wheel, so a full raid fits. Turn on **Grow with the number of rows** on the Window page of the options if you'd rather the window grows instead. Dragging a growing window's height with the grip gives it a fixed height again.
