# Combat log and disk space

*Logging raids and keys automatically, without filling your drive.*

Logs are what sites like Warcraft Logs read. CZ Meter can start the combat log for you, so you don't forget.

### Automatic logging

On the **Combat log** page of the options, pick when to log:

- **Off** (the default)
- **Raids and Mythic+**
- **All dungeons and raids**

If Advanced Combat Logging is off, CZ Meter warns you, since log sites need it. Turn it on in Esc > Options > Network.

![Starting the combat log automatically](https://ctrlshiftzed.com/assets/img/shots/czm-options-combat-log.webp)
*Starting the combat log automatically*

### When it stops

When CZ Meter starts the log, it stops it again when you leave the instance or log out. It never touches a log you started yourself with `/combatlog`.

### Disk space

Log files can reach several GB after a few raid nights, and addons can't delete files. So CZ Meter counts the hours it has logged and reminds you to clear old files from your Logs folder, `World of Warcraft\_retail_\Logs`.

- The reminder comes after **10 hours** of logging by default. Change it, or turn it off, on the Combat log page.
- After clearing your old logs, click **I've cleared my logs** to start the count again.
