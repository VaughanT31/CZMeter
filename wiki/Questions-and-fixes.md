# Questions and fixes

*Answers to the things people ask most.*

**Why do share and the spell list fill in after the fight?**

In Midnight the game hides meter values from addons during combat. CZ Meter can still draw them, so bars and numbers stay live, but anything it works out itself (share, the spell breakdown, item levels) fills in once the fight ends.

**Why can't I open another player's details in combat?**

For the same reason: their numbers are hidden from addons until combat ends. Your own details open any time.

**Why don't enemy rows show per second?**

The game's per second figure for enemies doesn't match their total, so it's left out. Identical adds are merged into one row.

**Do the numbers match other meters?**

Yes. Since Midnight every meter reads the same data from the game, and CZ Meter's totals match Blizzard's own meter.

**I can't change my window's height.**

The window is set to grow with the number of rows. Drag its height with the grip in the corner, or turn off **Grow with the number of rows** on the Window page of the options.

**A name is cut off.**

Turn off a column you don't need (share is the widest) from **Columns** in the window menu, or make the window wider.

**Will it slow my game down?**

It's built to be light: a few redraws a second in combat, and none out of combat unless something changed.

**Which game versions does it support?**

Retail (Midnight) only.
