# Skins

*Built-in skins, your own changes, and sharing a skin as a text string.*

### Built-in skins

| Skin | Looks |
|---|---|
| **Default** | Rounded, with item level badges |
| **Minimal** | Flat and see-through, with a compact toolbar |
| **Classic** | Textured bars and outlined text |

Pick one on the Window page of the options.

### Your own skin

Change any look setting (colours, bars, fonts, row size) and a custom copy of the skin is saved for you, named after the one you started from. The built-in skins never change, so you can always go back.

On the Skins page you can also **save** the current look as a new skin with your own name, or **delete** one. Windows wearing a deleted skin go back to Default.

### Sharing

1. On the Skins page, pick the skin and click **Export**.
2. Copy the text and send it to a friend.
3. They click **Import**, paste it in, and the skin is added to their list.

A skin only holds looks: no names, no numbers and nothing from your meter.
